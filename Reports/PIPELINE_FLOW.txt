ALGORITHM DETAILS
=================

CVE Mapping (TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity):
1. Build query: "vendor product service"
2. Transform to TF-IDF vector
3. Calculate cosine similarity with all CVEs
4. Filter by threshold (0.15 for OT, 0.20 for IT)
5. Filter by firmware version range
6. Return top matches

Risk Scoring (CVSS v3.1 Environmental):
1. Temporal = CVSS × exploit_maturity(EPSS) × remediation(KEV)
2. CIA = (C×0.20) + (I×0.30) + (A×0.50)  [OT weighting]
3. Environmental = Temporal × CIA × asset_criticality × 10
4. KEV bonus = Environmental × 1.10 (if KEV=1)
5. Suricata = Environmental + (alerts × 1.5)
6. Final = min(score, 10.0)
